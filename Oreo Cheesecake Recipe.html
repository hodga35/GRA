NO Bake Oreo Cheesecake

This Oreo cheesecake is a rich and creamy dessert with a crunchy Oreo crust and smooth cookies- and cream filling. It is topped with crushed Oreos for an extra chocolatey crunch.

Ingredients

For The Crust

  • 24 Oreo cookies, crushed
  • 5 tbs unsalted butter, melted

For the Cheesecake Filling

  • 16 oz cream cheese, softened
  • 1/2 cup powdered sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 1/2 cups heavy whipping cream

Intructions

  1. Mix crushed Oreos with melted butter. Press into the base of a springform pan. Chill while you make the filling.
  2. Beat cream chees, powdered sugar, and vanilla until smooth and creamy.
  3. In a seperate bowl, whip heavy cream until stiff peaks form.
  4. Gently fold the whipped cream and chopped oreos into the cream cheese mixture until well combined.
  5. Spoon the filling over the crust and smooth the top. Refrigerate for at least 4 hours or overnight until set.
